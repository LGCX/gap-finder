# MCS Redeveloped Installer Scheme — Evidence & Audit Manager

**One line:** Compliance evidence tooling for UK renewable installers moving onto the
redeveloped MCS Installer Scheme during 2026–27.

## Killing gate — G2 (feature of someone's roadmap)
The installer's job-management platform *is* the dominant platform in their stack, and
one already ships MCS workflow as a named feature. **Payaca** markets itself as running
"quoting, scheduling, **compliance** and payments" for solar/heat-pump/battery/EV
installers with tools tailored to "processes like **MCS certification** and DNO
applications" — https://payaca.com/us · https://payaca.com/uk/blog/redeveloped-mcs-installer-scheme

**Commusoft** already sells the audit-trail half: digital forms and checklists creating
"a complete audit trail" with instantly accessible compliance records —
https://www.commusoft.com/en-us/features/asset-maintenance-software/ ·
https://www.commusoft.co.uk/industry/renewable-energy-software/

Simpro sits above both. Any evidence-manager built here is one sprint on a competitor's
existing roadmap.

## The forcing function is weakening, not strengthening — B1
Unusually, the scheme redevelopment **reduces** the paperwork burden it would have sold
against. MCS's stated core philosophy shift is away from "excessive paperwork and
Quality Management Systems" toward the delivered quality of the installed system, and
assessment frequency becomes **risk-based** — consistently compliant installers get
*fewer* assessments. MCS is also staffing a Customer Relations / Account Manager
function to walk installers through ongoing compliance itself.
- https://mcscertified.com/installers/redeveloped-installer-scheme/
- https://niceic.com/views/redeveloped-mcs-installer-scheme-what-are-the-key-changes-and-benefits/
- https://www.installeronline.co.uk/news/mcs-invests-in-new-roles-to-improve-quality-outcomes-in-the-small-scale-renewables-sector/

Rollout is also phased across 2026 into 2027 per certification body, so there is no
single deadline to sell against.
