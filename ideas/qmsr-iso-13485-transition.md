# FDA QMSR / ISO 13485 Transition Tooling

**One line:** QMS conformance tooling for device makers now that FDA's Quality
Management System Regulation replaced the old QSR and incorporates ISO 13485:2016 by
reference.

## Killing gate — G1
QMSR took effect **2026-02-02**, replacing 21 CFR Part 820's QSR and pulling in
ISO 13485:2016; FDA has also changed its device inspection approach to match.
- https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/quality-management-system-regulation-qmsr
- https://www.ropesgray.com/en/insights/alerts/2026/02/a-qmsr-state-of-mind-fda-adopts-new-inspection-approach-for-medical-devices
