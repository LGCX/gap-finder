# TSCA §8(a)(7) PFAS Reporting

**One line:** Retrospective PFAS manufacture/import reporting submissions to EPA.

## Killing reason — it is an event, not a workflow
TSCA §8(a)(7) submissions are due **2026-10-13** for most manufacturers, with small
businesses reporting solely on imported PFAS-containing articles given until
**2027-04-13**. It is a lookback submission covering 2011–2022, not a recurring
operational obligation.
- https://www.epa.gov/assessing-and-managing-chemicals-under-tsca/tsca-section-8a7-reporting-and-recordkeeping

That fails B4 (pain frequency — annual-or-less, not daily/weekly) and destroys E2
(expected churn): every customer cancels the month after they file. A product whose
entire market disappears on a known date is a consulting engagement wearing a SaaS
costume.

Compounding it, the buyer already routes this through large EHS/regulatory vendors and
outside counsel, so there is no unclaimed budget line either.
