# CMS §6225 Provider-Based Attestation Manager

**One line:** Attestation preparation and filing for off-campus outpatient departments
under CMS's new mandatory provider-based attestation process.

## Killing gate — G2, stated outright by the regulator
CMS is **building the submission system itself**. The proposal requires attestations
signed by an authorised official identified in PECOS and submitted through a
"CMS-standardized electronic system, which is currently under development." When the
regulator ships the portal, there is no portal business.
- https://www.foley.com/insights/publications/2026/07/off-campus-outpatient-departments-cms-proposed-rule-for-the-new-mandatory-attestation-process/
- https://www.cms.gov/files/document/proposed-section-6225-attestation-format.pdf

## Second fail — B2
This is a **proposed** rule (July 2026), not a final one. No effective date, no
penalty schedule, and comments still open.

Buyer is also wrong for a solo founder: hospitals buy through Epic/Cerner-adjacent
procurement and revenue-cycle vendors, failing A5 and G5.
