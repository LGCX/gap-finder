# HFC Leak-Repair & Cylinder-Tracking Compliance (AIM Act subsection (h))

**One line:** Leak-inspection, repair-deadline and refrigerant-recordkeeping tracking
for owners of HFC systems, because EPA's ER&R rule took effect 2026-01-01.

## Why it looked good
EPA's Emissions Reduction and Reclamation (ER&R) program under AIM Act subsection (h)
brought leak repair, leak inspection and the associated **recordkeeping** into force
on **2026-01-01** for appliances holding ≥15 lb of HFCs (GWP ≥53). New commercial /
industrial refrigeration installs ≥1,500 lb must carry automatic leak detection from
2026; existing equipment by 2027. A container-tracking system for cylinder recovery
is part of the program. Already in force, penalty-backed, recurring in the workflow.
- https://www.epa.gov/system/files/documents/2026-01/er-r-fact-sheet-leak-repair-2026-01-13_1.pdf
- https://fexa.io/epa-final-rule-hfc-management-aim-act/
- https://www.bdlaw.com/publications/epa-finalizes-hfc-emissions-reduction-and-reclamation-program/

## Killing gate — G1 (big incumbent serving this exact buyer, this exact job)
Refrigerant compliance tracking is an established, well-funded category, not a gap:
- **Trakref**, the category's most established platform, now part of the **Fexa**
  facilities platform — https://fexa.io/fexa-trakref/
- **Accruent Verisae** (Accruent is a Fortive company), built for multi-site grocery /
  retail / food service refrigerant tracking — https://energy.toolsinfo.com/tool/accruent-verisae
- **ServiceChannel** and **SafetyCulture** both carry refrigerant-compliance modules —
  https://safetyculture.com/apps/refrigerant-compliance-management-software
- **Sphera**, **Facilio** — https://facilio.com/blog/best-refrigerant-compliance-management-software/

## Also fails C2 (incumbent shape) — no bottom-up wedge left
The rubric wants demo-gated enterprise incumbents to undercut. The bottom of this
market is already cheap and self-serve, which is C2 = 1:
- **RefriComply** from **$29/mo** — https://www.refricomply.com/blog/best-refrigerant-tracking-software-2026/
- **FMHero** free tier, $25/user/mo
- **RefriTrak**, positioning itself as "the professional standard for AIM Act Subpart C
  compliance — built for 2026" — https://www.refritrak.com/en

A new entrant would be the tenth vendor into a category where the incumbent floor is
$29/mo and the ceiling is owned by Fortive. There is no price or positioning gap.
