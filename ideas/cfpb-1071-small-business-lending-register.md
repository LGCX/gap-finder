# CFPB §1071 Small-Business Lending Register

**One line:** Data collection and register-filing tooling for lenders covered by the
CFPB's small-business lending data rule.

## Killing reason — the forcing function moved away, twice
The 2026 Final Rule (published 2026-05-01, effective 2026-06-30) **replaced the tiered
compliance dates with a single compliance date of 2028-01-01**, with first registers
not due until 2029-06-01. This is exactly the pattern the rubric's B2 warns about:
regulators defer, and this one has now deferred twice.

Worse for market size, it **raised the coverage threshold from 100 to 1,000 covered
credit transactions** in each of the two preceding years, cutting the covered
institution population sharply and pushing the remaining buyers toward the size where
they already own a core banking / compliance vendor.
- https://www.consumerfinancialserviceslawmonitor.com/2026/05/cfpb-issues-final-section-1071-rule-narrower-scope-later-compliance-date-and-a-leaner-data-collection-and-reporting-regime/
- https://www.gtlaw.com/en/insights/2026/5/cfpb-final-rule-narrows-small-business-lending-data-collection-requirements
- https://www.consumerfinance.gov/1071-rule/

Building in 2026 against a 2028 deadline, for a shrinking covered population, in a
segment where large regtech vendors bundle reporting into the core system, is not a
gap a solo founder should take.

## Watch date
**2027-Q1** — if the 2028-01-01 date survives to within 12 months and the CFPB has not
narrowed scope further, re-open and hunt specifically for the sub-$1B-asset credit
unions that have no core-vendor module. Until then, dead.
