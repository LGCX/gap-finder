# SQF Edition 10 Transition Readiness

**One line:** Gap-assessment and evidence tooling for food sites moving from SQF
Edition 9 to Edition 10 (weighted core clauses, mandatory food-safety-culture
assessment plan, 40-day CAPA closure).

## Second, independent fail — B2 (forcing-function durability)
Not in force. **Mandatory Edition 10 audits start no sooner than 2027-01-02**, and the
date is contingent on completing GFSI benchmarking, so it can slip further. Edition 9
remains valid and fully recognised until then. The rubric explicitly scores down
pending rules because regulators and scheme owners defer constantly — and this one
carries its own stated slip risk.
- https://blog.foodsconnected.com/sqf-edition-10-guide-food-safety-leaders
- https://www.eurofinsus.com/assurance/food/sqf-edition-10-changes/

Transition-window products also die when the window closes — the whole value is a
one-off migration, which is E2 churn ≈ 1.
